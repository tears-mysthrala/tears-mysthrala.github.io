## Prompt final

Create an original wide landscape illustration for a deeply personal Japanese mythology inspired website. Art direction: contemporary Japanese woodblock print meets meticulous ink illustration, elegant restrained fantastical nighttime scene, textured ink and visible paper grain, cinematic composition, no UI or lettering. Navy midnight sky, desaturated lavender mountains, moonlight silver, restrained coral vermilion accents. A crescent moon high right, winding dark sea with delicate silver wave lines below, a distant rocky island. On the left foreground an elegant small biwa lute rests against coastal rocks, with very fine curved musical strings flowing into the scene, a subtle reference to Benzaiten (no goddess portrait). In the center-right sea, one majestic giant vermilion octopus inspired by Akkorokamui emerges among waves, graceful long winding tentacles, expressive but not cute, mysterious and calm rather than horror. High right clouds carry a circular arc of three small traditional thunder drums and subtle silver lightning, a reference to Raijin, no character portrait. The scene feels intimate, artistic, poetic, sophisticated, not corporate and not anime. Composition artwork concentrated on middle and right, dark negative space at left upper quarter, spacious margins, panoramic 3:2 or wide landscape image. Flat print colors, strong forms, fine ink details, no neon, no glow effects, no 3D, no lettering, no symbols pretending to be Japanese writing, no logos, no watermark. This is an artistic interpretation, not an authentic historical artifact.
